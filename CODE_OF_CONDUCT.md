# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, precise, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Critique ideas, code, and evidence rather than people. Assume good faith while remaining accountable for impact.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through the contact channel configured for the project. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation.
